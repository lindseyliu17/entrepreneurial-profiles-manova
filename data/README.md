# Data

`entrepreneurship.csv` is the dataset published by Saleem and Anwar (2023): survey responses from 276 business and management students at the University of Buraimi, Sohar University, and Dhofar University in Oman. Responses were collected anonymously; the `ID` column is a sequential respondent number.

## Source and citation

- Dataset: Anwar, I. (2023). *A multivariate dataset on profiling personality traits, social and cognitive determinants of Omani students' entrepreneurial intention* (Version 1) [Data set]. Mendeley Data. https://doi.org/10.17632/36snsp9dgp.1
- Data article: Saleem, I., & Anwar, I. (2023). A multivariate dataset on profiling personality traits, social and cognitive determinants of Omani students' entrepreneurial intention. *Data in Brief*, 109363. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2023.109363

The original questionnaire is available from the Mendeley Data page.
